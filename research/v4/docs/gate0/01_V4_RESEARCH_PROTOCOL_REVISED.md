## 1. Research objective, target population and limitations

**Primary scientific question (P):** On prospectively eligible, independently resolved event groups, does an adaptive, settlement-aware event-family model provide incremental point-in-time probabilistic skill over a precisely defined market-only baseline?

**Secondary economic question (E):** Among *actual manually filled* positions satisfying a frozen decision policy, does realized net return (payoff less paid premium, fees and other documented costs) provide credible evidence of a positive, reproducible executable advantage? P and E are separate: improving Brier score does not establish profitability; a lucky positive PnL streak does not establish better probability forecasts.

**Tertiary engineering question (R):** Can event discovery, quote snapshotting, rule parsing, protected prediction logging, manual fill reconciliation, settlement labeling, and versioned updating run day after day with recoverable and auditable provenance?

**Scope:** Initially ForecastEx via the existing IBKR Client Portal Gateway. CME/Kalshi are future venue plugins requiring independent adapters/rules/fees checks. Do not assume every venue is exposed via the ForecastEx endpoints. Eligible **families** are discovered from current, tradable contracts with sufficient observed features and unambiguous final-outcome semantics; family selection is made via a preregistered feasibility rubric, NOT by selecting historically profitable contracts. The V3 weather-alpha domain remains retired/passive-watch; V4 does not retroactively reopen any V1–V3 hypothesis or trade the old weather route as proven alpha.

**Unit of prediction:** A frozen forecast about an *exact contract outcome* `Y_{event,threshold,side}` at a real point-in-time decision; `P(Y=YES)` is a proper probability, and `P(NO)=1-P(YES)` only if the contract definition and resolution geometry confirm strict complements.

**Unit of scientific observation:** one `outcome_event_id` at one prespecified as-of anchor, with one canonical YES target per eligible unique threshold. There is one underlying resolution value but potentially multiple threshold labels. Same event across relistings, rule revisions, venues and YES/NO sides is never an independent observation. Dependencies across events, including same-release and overlapping-window events, are assigned a `dependence_block_id` for inference; related outcomes share a `concentration_group_id` for exposure. See A1 in `07_G0_NORMATIVE_AMENDMENTS.md`.

## 2. Hypotheses, metrics, and estimand

**Primary prequential estimand:** prospective paired Brier improvement for a **precommitted daily adaptive updating policy** over a single canonical market baseline M0, not the most successful challenger selected later. For each event `g`, select exactly one admissible input snapshot at/before the G1-locked as-of anchor `T_g`. Define the enrolled threshold set `J_g^enrolled` using legal point-in-time information, and the scored subset `J_g^pair` as the thresholds with both on-time issued M0 and M2 (or issued fallback) and a venue-binding binary label actually observed and ingest-ready by the registered **scoring** cutoff `S_g`; a later label remains `LATE_LABEL` for the primary score even if usable in subsequent training. Define `d_g = (1/|J_g^pair|) * sum_{j in J_g^pair}[(p_M0(g,j)-y_gj)^2 - (p_M2(g,j)-y_gj)^2]` **only if** `|J_g^pair| > 0`; never divide by `|J_g^enrolled|` or assign a zero for missing counterparts. Average `d_g` equally over scoreable outcome events assigned to each pre-registered family, then over families by the **fixed G1-registered family-weight vector**. Duplicate side, poll, relisting or rule refresh does not gain weight. Use a registered `dependence_block_id` unit for uncertainty/splits; correlated releases may share a block even across families. Positive values indicate incremental probabilistic skill on the **predefined common-support cohort**, not all discovered contracts. Details, missingness and fallback: A1/A4 in `07_G0_NORMATIVE_AMENDMENTS.md`.

**Primary selection discipline:** before prospective outcomes, lock the family roster, event-level anchor schedule and offset `T_g`, forecast issuance deadline `D_g` (which may be later than `T_g`), deterministic last-complete snapshot received/ingest-ready no later than `T_g`, target eligibility, common support, registered score-maturity cutoff `S_g` (for scoring **only**), dependence blocks, fixed family weights and one formal terminal look. G1 may fix numeric timing values using outcome-blind feasibility. **All M0/M2/fallback inputs use the same `T_g` cutoff; each actual forecast issuance time `I_gj,M0`, `I_gj,M2` or `I_gj,fallback` is separately persisted and must be <= `D_g`.** No information first available after `T_g` enters a forecast even when issuance follows the anchor; an admissible post-anchor issuance must still precede any known/determined outcome or venue-review disqualification as of issuance. Do not substitute a late snapshot or retrospectively issue a fallback. Keep one enrolled-inventory denominator for coverage, and for each scored event use **only eligible paired, label-matured thresholds** `J_g^pair` (rather than all enrolled thresholds) in the mean. If `J_g^pair` is empty, that event contributes no score and has an explicit exclusion reason; retain missing/censored thresholds in the enrollment and coverage counts. An actually issued, on-time, preregistered M0-shrinkage fallback is paired with M0 and yields zero difference. Record missing M0, missing/on-time M2, no cutoff snapshot, outcome-known-at-issuance, void and late-label counts separately. No account fee, budget, edge, order, execution or realized return gate enters the **predictive** scoring mask; those gates only affect *trading*.

**Secondary predictive analyses:** clipped log loss, reliability/calibration, lead-time/family slices, explicit common-support coverage, missingness by type and predeclared dependency-block sensitivity. Use exactly one M0 for a verified $1 complementary pair: `p_M0,YES = (bid_YES+ask_YES)/2` on a synchronized two-sided YES book; NO is consistency check only. Missing/locked/crossed/one-sided/asynchronous quotes receive deterministic exclusion records and are never reconstructed after outcomes. For any future unverified payout structure, HOLD until a separately specified rule exists.

**Secondary economic estimand:** net reconciled cash return on **matched, matured manually filled** positions under a prospectively frozen decision policy: `sum(net broker-matched cash PnL) / sum(actual all-in purchase outlay)` over the same matured cohort, undefined when the denominator is zero. Book all premiums/fees/adjustments/coupons/refunds and only a **single** confirmed netting credit for a valid ForecastEx offset. Separate unmatched, partial, canceled and pending positions; do not infer fills or PnL from quotes. Report complete decision/attempt/fill/decline/settlement counts, deployed spend, drawdown and unresolved exposure. Positive realized return is descriptive until a separate predeclared economic inferential comparison is supported.

**Statistical claims:** Default all daily dashboards and frequent looks are *monitoring*, not repeated unrestricted significance tests. A formal positive-skill or positive-alpha claim requires a pre-registered prospective evaluation window, a specified stopping rule / alpha-spending or valid sequential test, dependence-aware uncertainty, and costs/selection effects. Until then: descriptive exploratory performance only.

## 3. Event and settlement semantics gate (must precede scoring)

For each proposition preserve the stable `outcome_event_id`, versioned `contract_target_id`, cross-release `dependence_block_id`, shared `concentration_group_id` and audited conid/relisting aliases. Do **not** include schedule time, venue, rule version or conid in the outcome ID. Persist rule identity and version, exact threshold/operator/rounding, period/unit, release and trade-stop, payout/exceptional clauses, source URL/content hash, and receipt/ingest-ready times. Conflicting aliases or multiple conids for one side => HOLD instead of input-order overwrite.

**Required family-specific source-state machine before forecast or executable recommendation:** `INSUFFICIENT_OR_CONFLICTING`, `OPEN_UNDETERMINED`, `PARTIAL_BOUND_YES_IMPOSSIBLE`, `PARTIAL_BOUND_NO_IMPOSSIBLE`, `KNOWN_YES`, `KNOWN_NO`, `VENUE_UNDER_REVIEW`, `VENUE_FINAL_BINARY`, `VOID_OR_EXCEPTIONAL`. Each transition must reference observed, ingested evidence as-of the prediction time; partial bounds are binding only when monotonic/irreversible under the pinned contract rule. Provisional or revised crossings are not silently treated as final; known outcomes are out of primary predictive/eligible-action cohorts. A still-undetermined proposition is predicted under any supported bound. Label admission later requires venue-binding evidence observed and ingest-ready **before the training checkpoint**, not merely source-effective timestamp. Venue corrections append versioned audit events. See A2.

**High-priority predictive holds (rule, evidence and as-of legality) versus trade-only holds (costs and execution):**
- Official settlement-source state already implies or contradicts the outcome; collect when available and distinguish *what was observable then* from final hindsight. V3 Study 2's `76/78` apparent positive quotes showed why this matters.
- Unknown official trading status or venue suspension is a trade hold. The primary *forecast* cohort separately requires its registered eligible market-status and legal, quality-qualified `T_g` input; **account trade access is never by itself a scoring exclusion**.
- Rules unresolved/changed, unsupported or ambiguous threshold semantics, event date differs from trading date, source release time confusion, or broker-conid mapping ambiguity.
- Paired YES/NO quotes are mirrors of one underlying book (observed in V3): do not treat them as two independent datasets or additive arbitrage without independent verified order/execution semantics.
- Unknown outcome semantics or payout geometry holds forecasting if the binary target itself cannot be identified; unknown account fee/fee upper bound, insufficient remaining budget or mismatched open orders **only hold trading**, not an otherwise legally issued paired M0/M2 forecast. Missing, stale or unsynchronized bid/ask/size and model-feature quality govern predictive availability by the G1 scoring rules; unsupported per-trade calibrated bounds govern only actionable suggestions.

The normal response to an unknown gate is `HOLD/NO_TRADE`, not a guessed probability. A deterministic status guard does not retrospectively change a frozen prior prediction; corrections append to the record.

## 4. Candidate and time selection (pre-outcome)

Discover broad live category trees, record all markets encountered (including skipped) and store a *scan manifest*. Each poll has **two independent evaluations**, both with versioned disposition records: (i) *predictive enrollment/issuance* at the registered `T_g`/`D_g`, based on family, exact target semantics, observed source state, admissible synchronized book and feature availability; and (ii) *trade-readiness* after prediction, based on trading access, account/session status, valid executable ask/size, fees, budgets and risk reservation. An unknown account fee or exhausted budget **does not prevent an otherwise legal paired forecast from being scored**. The trade-only ranking below cannot alter the predictive cohort. For trade-readiness only:

1. Eligibility: currently open for trading, trading access allowed, known payout, supported probability lower bound, valid REALTIME executable ask with size, acceptable quote age, sufficient time before last-trade cutoff, not previously resolved/retracted, no unresolved operational outage.
2. Prioritize nearest *tradable and not yet determined* event; **candidate initial horizon 30 min–24 h**, adjustable from data-readiness evidence **before** outcome-driven optimization. A same-day outcome may have an official release occurring well after last trade; do not equate expiry, release, payout and trade-stop.
3. Group the same underlying event across thresholds and YES/NO; enforce one-event concentration rules.
4. Rank by expected net edge after conservative probability bounds and executable ask/fee; tie-break based on stable ids to make the selection reproducible. Never require a purchase; save `NO_TRADE`/`HOLD` records.

**Primary forecast cohort is separate from trade readiness.** Freeze its enrollment at `T_g`, issue any M0/M2/fallback by `D_g`, record actual issue times and check source state at issuance. Score only the registered paired matured threshold subset, while separately reporting every enrolled threshold and missing category. Unknown account fees, limited risk budget, missing broker access, discretionary orders, execution and edge ranking cannot remove legal forecasts from scoring. Preserve item-level predictive and trade-only dispositions with evidence.

**No contract-specific learning.** An event-family model estimates a resolution distribution or outcome probability; the contract layer maps its strike/side and rules to `P(YES/NO)`. A shared baseline, calibration and risk layer operates across families. Highly heterogeneous (temperature / CPI / index futures) families may require different feature extraction and likelihood models. Pool or hierarchically share only when scientifically defensible.

## 5. Models and cold start

- `M0`: **one** locked market-only forecast for a verified ordinary complementary $0/$1 binary proposition, `p_YES=(bid_YES+ask_YES)/2` using a contemporaneous quality-qualified YES two-sided book; NO book is a mirrored consistency check, not a separate forecast. Exclude/censor quotes with absent age/sync/size status as pre-registered at G1, never choose market side post hoc.
- `M1_family`: Probabilistic family-specific predictive distribution/regularized model using only actual available-time covariates; start with simple parameters or transparent priors, never fabricate an unvalidated precision.
- `M2`: Predeclared market + family blend with calibration, shrinkage to M0 when sample support is weak. Output `p`, uncertainty / conservative lower bound if supported by a declared and calibrated construction, and `model_id`, feature manifest and hash.

Cold start: A market-only benchmark or a clearly flagged experimental family model may predict before enough independent labels exist, but a **positive-edge recommendation** cannot be justified merely by an unsupported numerical confidence interval. If usable forecasting support is missing, output forecasts for research and `NO_TRADE`. First small real orders (after Gate 3) require exact pricing, costs, status, model provenance, conservative evidence policy, and human approval; do not assert an empirically validated alpha.

**Numerical constraints:** for nested strict exceedance strikes on one resolution, probabilities must be non-increasing as the strike increases; YES/NO complementarity must hold when rules define complements. Enforce via coherent underlying CDF/distribution or constrained projection, not independent binary fits that contradict each other.

## 6. Live decision and human execution policy

For a binary contract with verified $1.00 settlement payout, per-unit theoretical long-YES value before other reserve is `p_yes - (ask_yes + all_applicable_fees)` (and analogously for NO); this is a model-based expectation, not proof of alpha or a promised fill. Market/fee terms must be contemporaneous and total all-in cash exposure <= $1.00 **per purchased contract**. Quote-derived EV is a **decision-screen estimate** only, not booked PnL.

To recommend a trade, require a **supported, preregistered** prediction interval `[L_Y,U_Y]`: use `L_Y` for YES and `1-U_Y` for NO (never `1-L_Y`), multiplied by verified $1 payout, then subtract the **maximum human-authorized limit price ceiling** (not just the currently displayed executable ask), verified upper-bound fees and execution reserve. Require the **strict** inequality `conservative_edge_at_ceiling > frozen_positive_margin` *at the limit ceiling*: equality is not sufficient and yields `NO_TRADE_EDGE_AT_LIMIT`. Record current ask separately as quote evidence. The intent's authorized limit must never exceed the exact ceiling screened, and its worst-case all-in reservation must not exceed $1 per purchased contract. An ask-pass/ceiling-fail case is `NO_TRADE_EDGE_AT_LIMIT`. A population-level Brier CI is not a per-trade probability bound. Without adequate independently justified family support/calibration, return `NO_TRADE_UNSUPPORTED`. Also require rule/status/data complete, available risk reservation capacity and no concentration breach. Initial edge floor, max quote age, supported interval method and reserve are `TBD` for outcome-blind G1 feasibility and signed G3 trade readiness; these values must be frozen before live use and may not be tuned against apparent profits in a protected evaluation stream.

**Suggested pilot guardrails, subject to explicit owner acceptance and Gate 3:** `max 1 unit/order`, `max $1.00 all-in per unit`, `max $1.00 outstanding exposure per underlying event group`, `max $5.00 potential daily loss`, `max $5.00 open exposure`, no leverage/automatic scale-ups, no chasing unfilled limit orders. These are proposed engineering defaults rather than claims about a safe personal loss level. Daily budget resets by declared local trading day; exposure counts open orders and live positions; multiple correlated contracts cannot escape a per-event cap by changing threshold/side.

**Human flow with atomic reservation:** Before any actionable recommendation is shown, use the **same ceiling price** for conservative edge and worst-case reservation; atomically persist durable snapshot/forecast/decision plus one-use manual order intent, expiry and **maximum all-in cash reservation** under event-group, daily and concurrent-open risk checks. No commit => no suggestion. Revalidate quote/source/IBKR session state at handoff; human alone enters a limit order with ceiling and size. Record outcome, including `SUBMISSION_UNKNOWN`, `CANCEL_REQUESTED` and `OPEN_UNRECONCILED`. Reservation **survives crash, midnight, UI handoff and unconfirmed cancellation** and only releases after trusted broker/manual reconciliation. Unmatched broker activity forces HOLD. All outlays remain <= USD 1 per purchased contract inclusive of fees. Private broker-order mappings stay outside public Git. **No programmatic order API, no paper trading.** See A5.

## 7. Continuous adaptive learning without leakage

**Primary adaptive-policy rule:** pre-register the entire daily update schedule and algorithm (`update_policy_id`) as the unit compared with M0. A group that has **already received an issued forecast and been scored** may legally enter a **later scheduled** train/tune/calibration update once its venue-binding binary label is observed, ingest-ready and mature by that later checkpoint, provided the new forecast is for a **different**, non-overlapping dependence block and the registered temporal/embargo rule allows it. Thus groups are disjoint **at each forecast checkpoint across its training boundary**, not permanently disjoint for the entire adaptive evaluation. The earlier group's original forecast and score remain frozen; protected labels of a later group cannot enter its own or overlapping-block forecast fitting, tuning or calibration. An outcome-driven change to the policy starts a **new** forward cohort, not an edited old effect. Primary analysis has a **single** formal terminal look with pre-registered enrollment/maturity and dependence-block uncertainty (G1 locks numerics outcome-blind); daily dashboard looks are descriptive only. Specific fixed-release challenger promotion is a *separate*, prospectively locked test with protected groups not used by either candidate or comparator. Training signoff and promotion signoff are distinct. See A4.

**Daily repeat cadence** (configurable/subject to API limits): scan and forecast every 5–15 minutes when relevant markets are active; append each eligible prediction; reconcile fills after manual activity; check newly released/officially finalized settlements at least daily or on observed release; update challenger **only** after new valid final labels become available. Intraday shadow refits may generate versioned descriptive forecasts, but **must not** alter the registered primary daily-update schedule, policy ID or its issued scoring stream. Changes to the algorithm, cadence or primary policy prompted by scores require a new forward cohort. Record outages and gaps. A high-frequency forecast is not a high-frequency independent label.

**Separate scoring maturity and training-label availability:** register a **scoring-only** maturity/censoring deadline `S_g` before outcomes. A threshold enters the primary paired Brier score only when its authoritative venue-binding binary label (or rule-verified binding source vintage) was effective, **actually received/observed and ingest-ready by `S_g`**, and its M0/M2 issuance and original input/identity/target guards pass. A subsequently obtained valid final label is recorded as `LATE_LABEL` for the fixed primary scoring cohort; it never silently backfills that score. For a scheduled model update at its **own** cutoff `U`, the same binding, receipt and ingest-ready proof must instead be **<= `U`**, along with exact target/rule/source-state legality, distinct dependence block, embargo, scheduled-update and original forecast-clock guards; `S_g` is **not** a training-admission deadline. All historical quote/features must have been received and ingest-ready by `T_g`; issued M0/M2/fallback must meet `D_g`, with pre-outcome state legal at actual issuance. **S04:** venue final stamped 12:00, first received+ready 12:08, score cutoff `S_g=12:06`: `LATE_LABEL` and no primary score; reject at training `U=12:05`, but allow consideration at `U=12:10` if all remaining guards pass. Provisional, review, void and exceptional payouts never silently become binary labels. A venue correction appends versioned corrections/restated scores without rewriting the original forecast, frozen primary scoring rule or old fit lineage.

**Immutable prequential ledger:** each prediction carries `model_id` pinned before its forecast issuance and input evidence cutoff; outcomes join later by appending a resolution event; never overwrite previous `p` or model version. Repeated daily or intraday retraining of candidate models is allowed, even if formal promotion is infrequent. All champion/challenger comparisons use paired forecasts on the same later events. Training cannot use the evaluated future event (or a future label for that event) through duplicated thresholds.

**Update algorithm v4.0:** event-family regularized model + explicit shrinkage/calibration; prepare immutable new checkpoint from finalized labels, record training cutoff, sample id hashes, seed, feature schema, source identity. Subsequent `v4.1` can implement incremental/online optimizer but first prove exact-data replay determinism and no training from unfinalized labels. Keep a frozen champion; candidate models may be trained frequently, but **promote** only at predeclared checkpoint if paired prospective event-group metrics improve with supported uncertainty and safety constraints, and no material data or cost regressions.

**Predeclared checkpoints (proposal):** diagnostics after 30 independent resolved event groups; formal candidate promotion only after a precommitted effective-sample/power/uncertainty rule (a working minimum of 100 independent groups may be tested for feasibility but is not a substitute for power analysis). Avoid optional-stopping claims: the **primary formal evaluation has exactly one pre-registered terminal look**, with any additional formal comparisons requiring a newly registered prospective cohort or an explicitly approved multiplicity plan. If no sufficiently convincing improvement: retain champion, continue data collection, possibly stop family allocation.

## 8. Data separation, Git release policy and audit integrity

The existing GitHub repo is public. For each scan, forecast, decision, actual trade and official settlement, write a durable local append-only event first, followed by **sanitized**, small, version-controlled exports under `research/v4/records/` (one daily immutable partition per type, or append-only journal with recorded digest chain). This allows rapid operation without a Git commit for every polling call. A daily automatic integrity job can stage outputs locally but **must not push** or publish unreviewed private material.

Key predictor feature values or a reproducible privacy-safe feature snapshot, immutable model input hash, event key, quote quality/status (and appropriately licensed quote fields), fees, prediction/model ID, decision reason codes, fill economics, official final label, and learning/checkpoint IDs must be reconstructible. Larger raw broker/provider payloads, source content subject to redistribution limits, and any sensitive execution records are kept outside Git in a content-addressed, access-controlled local archive, referenced by non-sensitive digests. A hash alone does NOT make lost raw data reproducible: maintain backed-up raw archive plus manifest and restore-test evidence.

**Explicit projection:** every scanned item has typed `scan_item_disposition`, including cases without a forecast, and a global health/error item may lack a contract or event. Public release uses a **recursive typed field whitelist** with provenance, license/use permissions and no nested metadata pass-through; raw provider payload and private broker mapping remain in backed-up authorized storage. Publicly authorized paired probabilities and labels can support *score replay*, but full time-travel replay depends on archived original input evidence and schema history. Never publish account IDs, bank data, tokens, session cookies, broker order IDs, IP addresses, addresses or authentication diagnostics. Public per-trade histories can reveal trading behavior; use stable random locally scoped `execution_ref` and publish only approved subsets. Verify data-provider redistribution terms before pushing live quotes. Future full model reproduction may require retained private raw source data; public repo alone may not suffice.

## 9. Monitoring, tripwires and responsible inference

Every day report: scanned/eligible/rejected markets, reasons, distinct underlying events, forecast counts, issued decisions and no-trades, approved trade attempts, fill/cancel count, actual all-in costs/positions, official labels obtained, unreconciled states, Brier/log-loss/calibration vs M0 (descriptive), realized PnL/drawdown, version lineage and outages. Always label maturity and sample size.

**Stop or downgrade to read-only** on: unknown settlement rule/change; stale or missing quote mode; widespread failure to capture pre-trade record; drift in conid/side map; unbalanced ledger; unreconciled positions; auth/token or sensitive-data leak; surprising deterministic positivity from already-known settlement state; family diagnostics consistently worse than baseline; budget breach; inability to verify actual fees/payout. Do not repair away a historical loss or relabel a previous prediction without an appended correction.
